# BM-TEST: Finding Gratitude in Everyday Life

[[CB1 id=gratitude-rich-teaching]]
## A moment to notice

In the hustle and bustle of everyday life, it is easy to rush past the small gifts right in front of us. Yet even an **ordinary moment** can become an occasion for *thanksgiving*. We can pause, notice what has been given, and bring our thanks to God.

Gratitude does not require a perfect day. It is not a way of pretending that suffering is pleasant. Instead, it is a simple practice of acknowledging God's goodness *while remaining truthful* about our circumstances.

A gentle reminder: start with what is actually there.  
Then let that observation become a prayer.

> BM-TEST: Gratitude begins with noticing, not with pretending.

- Notice one ordinary provision.
- Remember someone who has shown you care.

2. Thank God for it.
